Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking your perfect homestay

Our homestays include comfortable accommodation, daily breakfast, Wi-Fi access, and local host guidance. Some properties also offer additional meals, laundry services, and cultural activities.

Simply browse our available properties, select your dates, and complete the booking form. You'll receive instant confirmation and host contact details within 24 hours.

Free cancellation up to 48 hours before check-in. Cancellations within 48 hours are subject to a 50% charge. No-shows will be charged the full amount.

All homestays include breakfast. Many hosts also offer lunch and dinner options featuring authentic local cuisine. Dietary restrictions can be accommodated with advance notice.

After booking confirmation, you'll receive your host's contact information. Most hosts speak English and are available via phone, WhatsApp, or our messaging platform for any questions or assistance.

All our hosts are verified and background-checked. Properties meet safety standards with secure locks, emergency contacts, and 24/7 support. We also provide travel insurance recommendations.
